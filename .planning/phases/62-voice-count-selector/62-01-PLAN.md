<objective>
Add a user-facing "Voice Count" dropdown (1-24, default 24) to the standalone sampler
voice panel and wire its onChange to the already-built engine setter
`processorRef.setActiveVoiceCount(n)`. This is the entire VCOUNT-01 / VCOUNT-03 surface:
the selector lets the player pick how many voices are live, and because Phase 60 handles
ring-out + count-bounded allocation and Phase 61 fans the per-voice controls out across
`[0, activeVoiceCount)`, simply setting the count makes the controls and allocation follow
the new count immediately.

This is a pure GUI-wiring phase for the standalone app only (D-03 revised: the DAW-plugin
surface is deferred to a plugin-beta milestone). There is NO new audio behavior, NO DSP,
NO retrigger/culling logic, and NO plugin-surface code.

Purpose: Give the player the user-facing control over polyphony that Phases 60 and 61 built
the engine and control plumbing for.
Output: A working "Voice Count" ComboBox in the standalone sampler window, cloned from the
existing `recordModeBox` idiom, driving the engine count live.
</objective>
