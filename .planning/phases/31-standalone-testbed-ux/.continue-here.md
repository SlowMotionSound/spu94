<context>
The big debugging story this session: ADSR was working correctly the entire time (confirmed via stderr diagnostics showing engine level at 37% of max during sustain). The problem was the knob-to-shift mapping — PS1 shift values have an exponential relationship to time, and linear/quadratic mappings crammed all the audible range into the last few percent of the knob. Power curve (knob^0.55) solved it by concentrating the musical sweet spot (50ms-3s) in the middle 40% of the sweep.

The ADPCM codec amplitude loss (32131-peak input → ~1000-peak voice output) was investigated but turns out to be expected 4-bit codec behavior — the codec itself roundtrips at 99%, but the Gaussian interpolation + pitch counter stepping + ADSR multiply compound to reduce effective amplitude. Not a bug.
</context>
