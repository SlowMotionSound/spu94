<decisions_made>

- **Three-engine architecture, not dual.** [0]=smooth, [1]=scratch (mute,
  for target register computation), [2]=instant (audible). Engine Blend
  crossfades [0]/[2] continuously. Different goal from the abandoned
  dual-engine crossfade (which was for transition smoothing, only ran during
  morph CHANGES, and sounded like "two algorithms blending"). This new
  arrangement is *intentionally* both-engines-always-running for creative
  blend.
- **Granular Haze feeds reverb INPUT, not output mix.** Pre-feeding
  stretched echoes of the reverb tail into the reverb input creates a
  self-reinforcing texture. Cleaner than parallel layering because the
  grain content goes through the reverb processing.
- **Paulstretch-style Freeze.** Naive granular slowdown (fractional-rate
  playback) sounds like tape-pitch-down. Fix: keep grain playback at unity
  rate (no pitch shift); advance a separate **playhead** through the buffer
  at the freeze-controlled rate. Time elongation without pitch change.
- **Soft limiter, not runaway killer.** User wanted to preserve sustained
  feedback as a creative effect. Side-channel limiter bounds L/R divergence
  (the "blow out one ear" pain) while leaving mid content untouched so
  feedback can build up musically.
- **Side-limiter knee≠ceiling.** `tanh(side/0.125) * 0.06` — knee at 0.125
  (tanh feels right kicking in here), asymptote at 0.06 (harder
  redistribution than knee alone). Approved by ear after a 0.075 attempt.
- **L/R slider clamp = 4096.** Factory presets have ~1000–1500 unit L/R
  diffs on address registers, so 4096 leaves musical room without runaway
  divergence. Applied to 24 paired sliders only; single-channel registers
  (vIIR, vWALL, vCOMB1-4, vAPF1/2, dAPF1/2, mBASE) unconstrained.
</decisions_made>
