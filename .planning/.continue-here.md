| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |

Produced audible mid-high distortion. | blocking | Before implementing any SPU component, map the COMPLETE signal chain it lives in from psx-spx. Cross-check against DuckStation source. Never implement a stage without its neighbors. |
