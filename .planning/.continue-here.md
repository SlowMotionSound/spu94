<remaining_work>

BLOCKING: Split-Output Bus
The side limiter (kSideCeiling=0.06, kSideKnee=0.125) crushes ALL stereo content
because voice and reverb share one output bus from spu94_process(). The limiter
protects against reverb feedback squeals and MUST NOT be removed.

Fix scope:
1. spu94.h: add spu94_process_split() — writes voice (dry+adpcm+sampler) and
   reverb to separate buffer pairs
2. spu94_process.c: at the 4-bus mix (lines 267-276), write two sums instead
   of one. DAC model application is a design decision.
3. PluginProcessor.cpp: call split variant, side-limit reverb bus only, sum
   for final output. Voice stereo effects pass through clean.

Files: spu94.h, spu94_process.c, PluginProcessor.cpp
Size: ~2 tasks, half a session
