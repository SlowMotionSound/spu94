    Prepend ADR-0059 before ADR-0058 in docs/DECISIONS.md. Follow the Michael Nygard format established in the file header. Title: "Negative-phase volume sweep behavior and spec uncertainty."

    Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-05-22. Phase: 37 (Volume Sweep). Requirements: SWEEP-09, SWEEP-10.

    Context section must cover three areas of uncertainty:
    1. Negative-phase clamping boundaries: nocash says sweep with phase=1 "does probably increase to -7FFFh" (hedged -- "not yet tested"). The spec implies increase-toward-negative and decrease-toward-zero, but exact hardware behavior is unconfirmed.
    2. Phase bit in exponential decrease mode: nocash says phase "seems to have no effect" in exponential decrease. DuckStation implements this as phase_invert disabled when direction=decrease AND mode=exponential, but marks it "TODO: needs hardware test."
    3. Step sign inversion for negative phase: nocash implies the phase bit inverts the step direction (positive step becomes negative for phase=1 increase, meaning level moves deeper negative). This is the interpretation DuckStation uses but has not hardware-tested.

    Decision section must state SPU-94's chosen interpretation:
    - Positive-phase increase: clamp at +0x7FFF. Positive-phase decrease: clamp at 0.
    - Negative-phase increase: step is inverted (level moves toward -0x7FFF). Clamp at -0x7FFF.
    - Negative-phase decrease: step is inverted (level moves toward 0). Clamp at 0.
    - Exception: phase bit is ignored when direction=decrease AND mode=exponential (DuckStation interpretation).
    - Source: DuckStation spu.cpp VolumeEnvelope::Reset and VolumeSweep::Tick.

    Consequences section must note:
    - LOW confidence rating -- if hardware tests emerge contradicting this, a new ADR should supersede.
    - The negative-phase sweep is testable via the test_sweep unit tests but not yet validated against real PS1 hardware.
    - Risk: if the clamping boundaries are wrong, negative-phase sweeps could produce unexpected wrap-around or stall.

    Sources section: cite nocash psx-spx "SPU Volume and ADSR Generator" (sweep register bit 12 description), DuckStation spu.cpp VolumeEnvelope::Reset, DuckStation spu.cpp VolumeSweep::Tick.
  </action>
