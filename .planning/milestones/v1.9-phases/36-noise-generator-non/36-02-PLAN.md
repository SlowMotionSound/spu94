    Status: Accepted, Date: 2026-05-22, Phase: 36 (Noise Generator NON), Requirement: NON-01, NON-06, NON-09

    Context section covers three ambiguities:
    1. The LFSR polynomial: nocash documents "ParityBit = Bit15 XOR Bit12 XOR Bit11 XOR Bit10 XOR 1" which is XNOR (the final XOR 1 inverts). DuckStation uses a 64-entry lookup table. SPU-94 verified mathematical equivalence of both approaches.
    2. Initial seed: nocash does not document it. DuckStation uses 1. Seed=0 is absorbing (LFSR stuck at zero forever). SPU-94 adopts seed=1 from emulator consensus.
    3. ADPCM-fetch-during-NON: nocash does not explicitly state whether ADPCM decode runs for NON voices. DuckStation always runs SampleVoice (ADPCM decode) regardless of NON. SPU-94 follows DuckStation: ADPCM decode runs for side effects (loop flags, ENDX, address advancement).

    Decision section states SPU-94's choices for all three.

    Consequences section covers: deterministic noise sequence, LFSR maximal-length period, loop mechanics unaffected by NON toggle, voice can switch between NON and normal mid-playback without stale decode state.

    Sources: nocash psx-spx SPU Noise Generator page, DuckStation spu.cpp Reset() and SampleVoice(), PCSX2 SPU2 noise PR 4134.
